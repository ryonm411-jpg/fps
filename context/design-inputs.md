# Design Inputs

This file records ideas introduced by the additional design material supplied for the project.

## Input: demand vs. performance

The material highlights a "De Bruyne vs. TikTok" problem: a demand-heavy sports market may reward viral popularity more than football performance.

It proposes a 60% Demand / 40% Performance split to combine speculative excitement with a performance anchor.

## Input: performance scoring

The material proposes a position-aware 0–100 Performance Index with example weights for attacking, distribution, defending/goalkeeping, and discipline actions.

## Input: smoothing

The material proposes a rolling 10-game EMA to prevent one match from dominating the valuation.

## Input: dividends

The material proposes a weekly performance-based dividend, illustrated as per-share virtual/cash value. For this project, the MVP boundary remains virtual-money only; any adopted dividend mechanic must therefore be explicitly virtual unless the product scope changes.

## Implementation rule

These are design inputs, not automatically approved product requirements. The active decision state is recorded in `progress-tracker.md` and `market-model.md`.
