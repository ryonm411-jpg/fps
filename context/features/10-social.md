# Feature 10 — Social Layer

## Goal
Add lightweight community discussion around players and market events.

## MVP Features
- Player discussion posts
- Comments
- Likes
- Basic trader profile

## Boundaries
Social features do not execute trades, modify account balances, or directly alter player prices.

## Safety / Abuse Controls
- Rate limits
- Basic moderation/reporting flow
- Server-side authorization for edit/delete actions

## Acceptance Criteria
Users can discuss a player or market move while trading and accounting remain isolated from social content.
